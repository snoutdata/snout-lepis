# Lepis router after the performance round: pgbench, aws-c7g.2xlarge-routed-p5

`LEPIS_BENCH_ROUTED=1 LEPIS_BENCH_WORKLOADS='select tpcb' bash lepis/bench/pgbench.sh 30 <clients> aws-c7g.2xlarge-routed-p5c-<clients>`, 2026-10-06, on sd-jyr090ed.
Host: c7g.2xlarge, Debian 13 arm64, 8 CPUs. postgres:18, scale 20, 30 s each. Lepis: the router (a one-node catalog), pool_mode session and transaction. PgBouncer docker.io/edoburu/pgbouncer:v1.24.1-p1 in session mode.
The raw pgbench output was lost when the host's tree was shipped again; the tables are as printed. The baseline is 2026-10-05-aws-c7g.2xlarge-routed.md (60 s) and -routed-c64.md.
